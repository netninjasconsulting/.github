## Hi, I'm Jeff 👋

I'm Jeff Kloythanomsup, and Net Ninjas is my solo consultancy in New York City. I've spent about 20 years in cloud, DevOps, and AI work, from cloud support to Fortune 500 migrations to founding my own startup.

I map how your systems connect before I change any of them. Then I build the migration, the platform, and the AI work as one piece. Your engineers run it after I leave, and the next step stays easy to see.

I do the discovery, the design, and the hands-on work myself. No hand-offs to a junior team, no slide decks in place of shipped work. I work remotely with teams across the US and the EU.

## How it works

1. **A short call.** 30 minutes on your current setup, your stage, and what's bothering you about it. You get a straight opinion, whether or not we work together.
2. **A one-page proposal.** Scope, deliverables, and a fixed price. If the honest answer is that you don't need me yet, the page says so.
3. **The work, then the handover.** I stay until it runs in production and your team owns it, with notes on what to watch and what comes next.
